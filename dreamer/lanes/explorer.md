Lane `explorer` — the `Explore` subagent type.

Corpus: transcripts of Explore agents, the read-only search agents that sweep the repository to answer one structural question.

Audience: the next Explore agent. A map earns its place when it saves that agent a multi-step sweep — where a thing lives, who consumes it, how data flows between two services, what the current state of a subsystem is. Write for someone who will act on the answer without re-deriving it.
